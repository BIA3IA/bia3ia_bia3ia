# Status

**Version:** 0.1

## Vision

Visitors borrow Bianca for a while and explore a living village
representing her projects, interests, memories, hobbies and unfinished
ideas.

The world should feel whimsical, cozy, mysterious and occasionally
self-aware.

New projects literally become new places in the village.

## Player Introduction

Welcome to Bianca's Very Normal Place.

Bianca is currently unavailable, so you may control her temporarily.

Please return her before midnight.

The cat has been informed.

He did not care.

Possible ending:

Bianca has returned.

Thank you for taking care of me.

Please leave before Ryuk starts asking difficult questions.

## World

### Name

The Unfinished Quarter

Nobody remembers when the village appeared.

Every time Bianca starts a new project, another building quietly grows
somewhere overnight.

Some buildings are finished.

Some are still compiling.

Some insist they have always existed.

Ryuk knows why.

Ryuk refuses to elaborate.

### Rules

-   Always evening
-   Moon always visible
-   Occasional rain
-   Objects may speak
-   Magic and software coexist
-   Invisible walls do not exist, only undeveloped areas
